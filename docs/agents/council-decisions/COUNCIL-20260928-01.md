Recommendation: adopt v1 for substantive ticket decisions and acceptance, while routine execution
continues within accepted scope. Deputy + required specialists first endorse a shared revision;
then the Chair and all participants review and explicitly endorse the same final revision.

Alternatives considered: keeping the previous single-Orchestrator workflow; convening the entire
roster for every edit; allowing a majority or Chair override after disagreement. The first misses
the user's requested Deputy/Team process; the second adds repeated work; the third contradicts
unanimity. The selected policy uses a declared, scope-based roster and evidence-based dispute handling.

The strongest counterargument is that unanimity can stall work or encourage conformity. The policy
keeps independent initial findings, visible objections and Needs evidence votes, two bounded
reconciliation rounds, and explicit unresolved-dispute escalation. Disputes never become an
approved recommendation merely because the Chair prefers an option. Unaffected work can continue.
