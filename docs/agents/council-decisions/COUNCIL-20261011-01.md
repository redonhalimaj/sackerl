junior brief and TEAM/AGENTS/PROGRAM/CLAUDE/agent README consistency, plus the mechanical current
renderer-version correction in the application-test-harness document.

explicit simulated labels and independent QA bound the claim to source lifecycle behavior.

accessibility, development build, full 908 and Stage 1 remain open.
