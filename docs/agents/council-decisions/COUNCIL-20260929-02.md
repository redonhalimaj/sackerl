The Chair framed the owner's latest simulator feedback as a priority decision for the next bounded

## Recommendation and evidence

The next single bounded action is BPA documentation refinement of provisional SCKRL-312 (optional
expiry entry for a reviewed receipt line) and SCKRL-305 (clear post-save continuation, exit and
return behavior). The refinement must expose, rather than settle, these contract questions:

- How unknown/no date, an explicit user clear and an entered date differ.
- Whether expiry is persisted by the generation/revision-guarded review save or carried at placement.
- Which confirmation and provenance the resulting stock expiry fact records.

A subsequent Council with Backend, Infrastructure, Frontend, Product and independent QA must settle
those questions before SCKRL-311 implementation. SCKRL-311 then precedes SCKRL-305. Independent
connected and native QA for SCKRL-304 and SCKRL-406 continues; both remain Review. SCKRL-308 remains
Ready for an independent Infrastructure lane and precedes SCKRL-307/309.

Evidence: the [current checkpoint](../../../status.md) records the owner's sample review, edit sheet,
save message and blocked placement; [SCKRL-304/305/308/311 scope](../../../features.md) and the
[SCKRL-310 review contract](../../features/sckrl-310-receipt-review-data.md) show no receipt-line
expiry field and deliberately disabled placement; the [SCKRL-406 contract](../../features/sckrl-406-expiry-provenance.md)
requires honest expiry provenance when stock is created. The screenshots do not establish save
persistence after reload, correction, conflicts, or native accessibility.

The strongest counterargument was that SCKRL-308 is Ready and delays to it can delay real receipt
capture. It remains a necessary independent prerequisite and may proceed in a separate lane, while
the owner-tested sample review cannot proceed to placement without the receipt expiry and placement
contract. Enabling placement immediately was rejected because SCKRL-311's atomic stock command and
SCKRL-305's accessible placement UI are not implemented.
